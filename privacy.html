Privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026.

jh-recruiting is a personal application used only by its developer, Maximus Tarnopolsky, to track his own job applications. No one else signs in to it.

What it accesses

  • Gmail (read): to find messages about his applications, such as confirmations, deadlines and interview invitations.
  • Gmail (compose): to create drafts for him to review. The application never sends email.
  • Google Calendar (events): to read and add events for his deadlines and interviews.

Where data goes

Data from Google is stored only on his own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Short excerpts of his messages may be processed by Google's Gemini API to extract dates and status changes.

jh-recruiting's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
